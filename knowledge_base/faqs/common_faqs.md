# Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What should I do if my modem lights turn RED?
**Answer**: A red optical/LOS light indicates physical fiber line signal loss. Power off the modem for 30 seconds. If the light remains red after rebooting, log a support ticket for ground line repair.

### Q2: How do I test speed without local network interference?
**Answer**: Connect a single test laptop via Cat6 ethernet directly to the primary modem port, disable all VPNs and background downloads, and run the test.

### Q3: What causes one-way audio on SIP calls?
**Answer**: One-way audio is usually caused by SIP ALG enabled on your firewall or UDP RTP media ports (10000-20000) being blocked inbound.

### Q4: How long does ground fiber splice repair take?
**Answer**: Standard SLA for field fiber cut repair is 2 to 4 hours from ticket escalation.
